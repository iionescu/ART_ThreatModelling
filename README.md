# ART_ThreatModelling
Ionut Ionescu's ART method of threat modelling

# History

As a security professional, I have been practicing modelling and, in particular, Threat Modelling (TM), in a variety of environments, since circa 2010.

As I started teaching Threat Modelling to people from a variety of backgrounds (security, software engineering, architecture, testing, risk and compliance, product, platform/DevOps, etc), I realised that I needed a way of thinking with broad applicability, that could work in many contexts.

While many books and articles talked about various methods, mnemonics and steps, there were not a lot of materials that showed people how to actually do Threat Modelling, in practice.

I created the ART (Asset - Risk - Threat) method of threat modelling as an easy to remember recipe for anyone to start threat modelling.
