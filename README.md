# ART_ThreatModelling
Ionut Ionescu's ART method of threat modelling

# History

As a security professional, I have been practicing modelling and, in particular, Threat Modelling (TM), in a variety of environments, from the turn of the 3rd millenium (the 2000's).

As I started teaching Threat Modelling to people from a variety of backgrounds (security, software engineering, architecture, testing, risk and compliance, product, platform/DevOps, etc), I realised that I needed a way of thinking with broad applicability, that could work in many contexts.

While many books and articles talked about various methods, mnemonics (like STRIDE, DREAD, PASTA, etc) and steps, there were not a lot of materials that showed people how to actually do Threat Modelling, in practice.

I created the ART [ Asset - Risk - Threat ] method of threat modelling as an easy to remember recipe for anyone to start threat modelling.

## Definition

ART stands for ASSET - RISK - THREAT.

It is an asset-centric method of threat modelling and it can be used in isolation, and in conjunction with other methods.
